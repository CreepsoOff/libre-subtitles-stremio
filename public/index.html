Libre Subtitles for Stremio

Enjoy a one-click way to install high-quality subtitles from libre-subs.fifthwit.net in your Stremio app. Pick your favourite languages and sources, we take care of the rest.

⚙️ Quick install

  1. Keep this page open while the add-on is running.
  2. Open Stremio → Addons → Community Addons.
  3. Use the button below to install or share the generated link.
